      Other sites or RSS feeds

      Feeds (.rss, .xml, /feed) work reliably. Telegram channels: add t.me/channel. Public channels need your Telegram account logged in (Auto-apply tab). Other pages are a best-effort scrape and switch themselves off if they keep failing. LinkedIn, BOSS直聘, 拉勾 and similar sites can't be read.

      What you want

      A job must mention at least one. English words also match Chinese (backend ↔ 后端). Leave empty to get everything.
      Jobs mentioning any of these are dropped, even if a keyword matches.
      Countries, cities or groupings like EU, APAC, Southeast Asia, 国内. A job passes if it hires worldwide or overlaps these, so Germany also matches “EU only”. Leave empty for anywhere.
      Only matters when you've set regions.

      More filters

      0 = no minimum. Roughly converted from other currencies.
      0 = no limit.
      A reworded repost of a job you got recently isn't sent again. 0 = off.
      The rest wait for the next check.

      AI

      Optional. Makes job cards cleaner, filters out posts from job seekers, and writes your applications.

      Auto-apply

      Sends a short application with your résumé to the contact a job post names: a Telegram handle (from your own account) or an email address.

      If you paste it here, the AI reads this instead of the PDF (cheaper and a bit more reliable).

      Which jobs to apply to

      Applications (AI-written or the template) and Job Radar's notices in Telegram use this language.
      With AI on, each job is scored against your résumé. 70 is a sensible start; raise it if the dry run shows poor matches.
      Optional with AI on. Required without AI, otherwise nothing is applied to.

      to

      Your Telegram account

      Needed to message hiring managers on Telegram (a bot can't), and to read public Telegram job channels. Get your API ID and API hash at my.telegram.org → API development tools.

      Telegram login needs an extra package. In a terminal, run pip install telethon, then restart Job Radar. (The Windows .exe already includes it.)

      Logging in creates jobradar-user.session next to your config. It gives full access to your Telegram account, so never share it.

      Email

      For jobs that only list an email address. For Gmail use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, and an App Password, not your normal password.
